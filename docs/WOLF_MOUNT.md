# Wolf tiny mount

Wolf extends the existing tiny-mount registry, saddle slot, direct steering and relative-size policy. The bundled definition permits a maximum rider/mount effective SCALE ratio of 0.76. Wolves must be adult. Any player can saddle a tamed wolf, regardless of ownership or rider size; wild wolves reject saddles with a specific message. Wolf Armor uses BODY and the saddle uses SADDLE, and both remain on dismount.

Use **Crouch + Use** with any held item or an empty hand to mount a wolf or other configured tameable. These are configured game actions, not fixed keyboard/mouse buttons. This gesture takes priority over feeding, equipment and item use, including holding a saddle on an unsaddled animal. Normal Use retains vanilla sitting, feeding and saddle equipment. Release Crouch before pressing it again to dismount. Every new ride, including returning to the same animal, protects the initiating Crouch press. Riding still checks relative size, available seat and the vanilla boarding cooldown. Any compatible player may ride a tamed wolf without changing its owner. Tiny Mounts can carry riders without saddles, but without a required saddle their own AI controls movement; no steering or pounce is granted. Vanilla horse/pig/strider controllers are not replaced.

Wild adult wolves allow uncontrolled taming attempts. Each sustained ride lasts 60 server ticks (three seconds); the first two attempts eject the player, and the third tames the wolf to that player. Dismounting a still-wild wolf, voluntarily or by ejection, makes it target that rider. Normal survival combat rules still apply. Partial rides do not count; completed attempts are transient, reset for another rider and are not saved across unloading/restarts. Bones remain a normal taming route. Once tamed, dismounting does not provoke aggression. Saddle-free riding still does not grant control after taming.

WASD controls the wolf using the rider's look direction. The server owns movement and attacks. A short Space press (up to three server ticks) attempts the wolf's normal forward attack; holding Space charges a pounce over ten ticks. The existing horse jump HUD shows charge. Release launches along yaw with bounded pitch-dependent vertical movement. The first valid target intersected during a pounce receives one normal wolf attack. The action cooldown uses the vanilla melee goal's 20-tick interval. Landing removes residual pounce sliding.

Commanded bites emit the native main-hand swing animation, including a missed tap and a pounce hit. The wolf advances the native swing clock on both sides and briefly enters the synced melee combat pose; this does not assign an AI target or create anger. Only a pose introduced by the command is cleared when the swing finishes, including after dismount. Resource packs can consume the native attack progress (`swing_progress` in EMF). The vanilla wolf model has no dedicated bite animation. Installed Fresh Animations 1.10.5 uses `is_aggressive` for combat poses but does not define a bite driven by attack progress; this integration activates those existing poses without inventing a jaw animation or requiring a resource pack.

The saddle uses the existing hand-authored leather pixel-art generator and manually adapted equipment geometry. It follows the wolf body animation and coexists with the vanilla armor layer. No image-generation assets are used.

Development validation covers shared mounting, owner and equipment preservation, effective-scale ratios, real damage attribution and transient commanded-attack scope. Additional two-player manual acceptance remains necessary for latency, team/PvP combinations and the complete betrayal interaction. No special gameplay tutorial is added.
