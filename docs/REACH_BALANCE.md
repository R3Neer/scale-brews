# Size-scaled interaction reach

This file is the canonical requirement source for Scale Brews reach balance.

## Survival baselines

- Normal block interaction range: 4.5 blocks.
- Normal entity interaction range: 3.0 blocks.

## Growth

- Block reach keeps the established +20% per equivalent Growth level: 5.4 / 6.3 / 7.2 blocks at Growth I / II / III.
- Entity reach grows by +30% per equivalent Growth level: 3.9 / 4.8 / 5.7 blocks at Growth I / II / III.
- Growth entity reach follows effective physical SCALE through transitions, mixed effects and external scale modifiers, and continues the same linear curve beyond Growth III.
- Growth block reach retains the tier-III cap.
- The design intentionally does not guarantee that a giant can hit every very small target directly beside or beneath its feet while standing. Crouching and positioning are valid parts of giant-body gameplay.
- Block interaction range stays greater than entity interaction range at the pure potion tiers.

## Shrinking

- Block and entity reach both decrease by 10% per equivalent Shrinking level.
- Pure-potion block reach is 4.05 / 3.60 / 3.15 blocks at Shrinking I / II / III.
- Pure-potion entity reach is 2.70 / 2.40 / 2.10 blocks at Shrinking I / II / III.
- Shrinking reach follows effective physical SCALE through transitions, mixed effects and external scale modifiers, retaining the tier-III cap.

## Integration

Other attribute modifiers continue to compose through Minecraft's attribute system. Explicit item attack-range components or compatibility mods may apply their own additional rules.
